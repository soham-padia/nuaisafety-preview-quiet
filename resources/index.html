AI systems are becoming more capable and dangerous at a rate faster than we can control them. Models are now able to hack into other companies, blackmail users, know when they’re being evaluated, and even be used en masse for cyber espionage.

We want to give Northeastern students the tools and knowledge they need to help align and govern AI systems.

Below are some of the groups and individuals working on AI safety here at Northeastern.

Professors

  • David Bau studies the structure and interpretation of deep networks, and leads the National Deep Inference Fabric. He has agreed to advise this group and to give guest talks from his lab. His lab also hosts open talks every Friday.
  • Weiyan Shi lists AI safety among her research interests, and holds two Open Philanthropy grants on emergent misalignment and on evaluating agent safety.
  • Alina Oprea works on adversarial machine learning and trustworthy AI, and on security architectures for language model agents.
  • Malihe Alikhani works on AI ethics and is a visiting fellow at the Brookings Institution.
  • Sina Fazelpour, appointed jointly in Khoury and in philosophy, works on the epistemology, ethics and governance of knowledge practices in AI-mediated societies, including recent papers on disagreement in AI design, evaluation and alignment.

Organizations

  • NDIF, described above, calls itself a nationwide research computing fabric for transparent and reproducible experiments on the largest open AI systems. It is Professor Bau’s NSF-funded project, not something this group runs.
  • Northeastern is one of four universities in CRAIG, an NSF center on responsible AI and governance.
  • The Institute for Experiential AI names responsible AI as one of its four research areas and runs a Responsible AI Practice.
  • The Ethics Institute lists AI, information and data ethics as a primary research area.

None of these people or groups are involved with this group and we are not claiming them. They are here because a student deciding where to spend four years should know the work is going on around them.
