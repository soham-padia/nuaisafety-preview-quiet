AI Safety at Northeastern

We are a student-led group preparing Northeastern students to help align and govern advanced AI systems. We run technical and policy fellowships, host talks from researchers, and support student research.

Three different problems. Three different kinds of people.

AI safety is not one problem with one kind of answer. The three routes people worry about most need different training, different rooms, and different arguments.

Loss of control

Systems that pursue something other than what we asked for, and that we cannot reliably stop.

Interpretability, evaluations

Concentration of power

AI that lets a small group hold power nobody else can meaningfully challenge.

Governance, law, policy

Biological misuse

Models that lower the barrier to designing a pathogen, and the defences against that.

Security, biology, public health

This is roughly how BlueDot's AGI Strategy course splits the problem. We are not affiliated with them. Their curriculum is open, and it is a good place to start reading.

What we do

Fellowships

Weekly reading groups on the technical and policy sides of AI safety, from the fundamentals to current research.

Guest talks

Talks from researchers in AI safety and interpretability, from across Boston and beyond.

Research

Support for members pursuing their own AI safety research, with feedback and collaboration from other members and Northeastern faculty.

Who this is for

AI safety needs more than just AI researchers and software engineers. Many of its most important problems concern policy, law, cybersecurity, biology, and psychology. Anyone interested in making AI go well is welcome!
